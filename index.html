Curator · Art Historian

David Bäcker is a curator and art historian working across contemporary art, exhibition-making and performative practices.

About

He has worked with museums, galleries, and cultural festivals on the development and realization of exhibitions and artistic projects.

His practice combines curatorial research and exhibition-making with experience in project organization, production, and art handling.

Bäcker studied art history in Berlin and Tel Aviv.
